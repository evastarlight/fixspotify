Fix your Spotify links

By simply replacing open.spotify.com with open.fixspotify.com in your shared links, fixSpotify ensures that your Spotify links display correctly and consistently.

About

Hi, I'm Eva, I'm currently the one maintaining fixSpotify. It was originally made by Max, who got fed up with Spotify links looking all broken on Discord and other platforms and built fixSpotify.

Then it got hit with a DMCA it didn't even deserve and went offline. I couldn't let it just die, so I asked Max if I could take it over, she said yes and now I run it.

It's running on a global network all over the world now so it should be quick pretty much anywhere.

Thanks for using it :)
